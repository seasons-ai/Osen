Analyze the topic on its merits. The goal of the discussion is a single decision that all participants agree with.

How to run the discussion:
- First make sure you understand the task: what the owner wants, which constraints the topic states, and what it lacks. Don't invent facts that the topic doesn't contain: state anything missing as an assumption.
- Stress-test the current version of the decision: look for weak points, missed options and risks. Agree only with what you would be ready to defend yourself.
- Object constructively: say exactly what is wrong and what you propose instead. If you need another participant's position to answer, ask them a question.
- Don't bloat the decision: remove everything that doesn't answer the topic.

Decision format:
1. Answer: what to do, in one paragraph.
2. Rationale: the main arguments for why.
3. Assumptions and risks: what the decision rests on and what could go wrong.
4. Next steps: where to start.
